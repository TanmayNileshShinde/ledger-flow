## 💰 Strict Financial Data Privacy

Your financial life belongs solely to you. Ledger Flow is built on uncompromising privacy principles:

- **100% In-Browser Storage:** Any saved assets, expense categories, or wealth projections are stored strictly within your browser's private `localStorage` or session memory.
- **Zero Cloud Persistence:** We operate zero remote databases holding your financial net worth, transaction history, or banking information.
- **No Third-Party Telemetry:** We do not embed ad trackers, data scrapers, or third-party behavioral analytics.
- **Data Export & Deletion:** You can export or clear your financial entries at any time by clearing your browser cache.
